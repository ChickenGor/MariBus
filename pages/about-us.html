About us

Making Malaysian buses easier to use.

MariBus brings route discovery, scheduled journeys and available live bus information into one map-first experience.

Our mission

Confidence from A to B

We want riders to understand which bus to take, where to board, where to exit and when a live vehicle may arrive—without needing to know the exact stop name first.

Built in Malaysia

MariBus is an independent project by Chicken Rice Developer Studio. Transit information depends on the availability and quality of operator and open transport data.
